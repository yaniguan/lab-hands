## 1. Object

`lab-hands` is the **Visuo-Tactile Manipulation & Motion Planning (`L1`)** component of the [Robot AI Auto-Lab](https://github.com/yaniguan/auto-lab). Within the `L0`–`L6` Brain/Body stack, `L1` bridges symbolic benchtop actions to collision-free physical execution: moving pipettes, tubes, and microplates across dense lab decks while framing visuo-tactile feedback for the "last centimeter" of manipulation, where depth cameras fail on transparent borosilicate glass, clear polypropylene tubes, and refractive liquid menisci.

### Inputs and Outputs (`demo/plan_to_well.py`)

- **Inputs**:
  - 1-based well indices (`1..96`, row-major or column-major) or alphanumeric SBS well names (`A1`–`H12`) on a standard `9 mm`-pitch 96-well plate (`PLATE_ORIGIN = (0.20, 0.05) m`).
  - Planar 2-link arm link lengths (`L1 = 0.30 m`, `L2 = 0.25 m`), aspiration source coordinate (`TUBE = (-0.05, 0.40) m`), axis-aligned bounding-box (AABB) deck obstacles (`OBSTACLES`), and deterministic random seeds (`0..9`).
- **Outputs**:
  - Analytical inverse-kinematics joint configurations $q = (q_1, q_2) \in \mathbb{R}^2$, collision-free joint-space waypoint trajectories `[q_0, ..., q_K]` generated by RRT and refined by randomized shortcutting, and summary metrics (blocked straight-line count, trial success rate, mean/max planning latency in ms, and raw vs. shortcut joint path length in radians).

### Scope Boundaries

- **Implemented today**: Pure-`numpy` 2D top-down kinematic + sampling-based motion planner (`fk`, `ik`, discrete link-AABB `in_collision`, `rrt`, `shortcut`) evaluated across `10` target wells $\times$ `10` seeds (`100` trials), paired with a curated engineering map of open robot arms, VLA/diffusion policies, transparent-object depth datasets, and optical tactile sensors.
- **Out of scope / next stages**: 3D workspace collision checking (Z-axis tip lift/plunge clearances), rigid-body dynamics and fluid slosh constraints, multi-DoF URDF kinematics (e.g., SO-ARM100 / ALOHA in MuJoCo), and learned closed-loop visuomotor or visuo-tactile policies (ACT, Diffusion Policy, OpenVLA, SmolVLA, Sparsh).

## 2. Data

### 2.1 Built-in Geometric & Labware Specification (`demo/plan_to_well.py`)

All geometry in the executable baseline is synthetic, self-contained, and anchored to ANSI/SLAS (SBS) microplate dimensions:

- **SBS 96-well plate geometry**:
  - `8` rows (`ROWS = "ABCDEFGH"`) $\times$ `12` columns (`1..12`), center-to-center well spacing `PITCH = 0.009 m` (`9.0 mm`), physical footprint `99 mm × 63 mm` between well centers (`A1` at `(0.20, 0.05) m` to `H12` at `(0.299, -0.013) m`).
  - Dual index-to-well mapping (`well_name(index, order)`): supports both `'row'` (`A1..A12, B1..`, e.g., `65 -> F5`) and `'col'` (`A1..H1, A2..`, e.g., `65 -> A9`) 1-based conventions.
- **Arm & cluttered benchtop layout**:
  - Base shoulder joint fixed at `(0.0, 0.0) m`; link lengths `L1 = 0.30 m`, `L2 = 0.25 m` (reachable radial annulus $[|L_1 - L_2|, L_1 + L_2] = [0.05\text{ m}, 0.55\text{ m}]$).
  - Source aspiration tube at `TUBE = (-0.05, 0.40) m` ($\|p\|_2 = \sqrt{0.1625} \approx 0.4031\text{ m}$).
  - Static AABB obstacles `(xmin, ymin, xmax, ymax)`:
    1. Tube rack wall: `(0.33, -0.08, 0.36, 0.12)` (bounds elbow sweeps to the right of the plate).
    2. Reagent bottle: `(0.15, 0.27, 0.24, 0.34)` (sits directly across the straight joint-space sweep from `TUBE` to the 96-well plate).
- **Evaluation suite**: `10` representative wells spanning plate corners and interior (`A1`, `A12`, `D6`, `E5`, `F5`, `H1`, `H12`, `C9`, `G3`, `B7`) $\times$ `10` RNG seeds (`0..9`) = `100` planning episodes.

## 3. Model

### 3.1 Planar 2R Kinematics (`fk`, `ik`)

- **Forward kinematics (`fk(q)`)**: Given joint angles $q = (q_1, q_2)$ in radians, computes the elbow position $e \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and pipette-tip position $t \in \mathbb{R}^2$:
  $$e = \begin{bmatrix} L_1 \cos q_1 \\ L_1 \sin q_1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad t = e + \begin{bmatrix} L_2 \cos(q_1 + q_2) \\ L_2 \sin(q_1 + q_2) \end{bmatrix}$$
- **Analytical inverse kinematics (`ik(p, elbow=1)`)**:
  - Computes cosine of elbow angle $d = \frac{\|p\|_2^2 - L_1^2 - L_2^2}{2 L_1 L_2}$; returns `None` if $|d| > 1$ (unreachable target).
  - Solves $q_2 = \text{elbow} \cdot \arccos(d)$ for `elbow` $\in \{+1, -1\}$ and $q_1 = \operatorname{atan2}(p_y, p_x) - \operatorname{atan2}(L_2 \sin q_2, L_1 + L_2 \cos q_2)$.
  - In `main()`, the solver queries `elbow = +1` first and falls back to `elbow = -1` if `q` is `None` or `in_collision(q)` is `True`.

### 3.2 Discrete Link-AABB Collision Checking (`in_collision`, `edge_free`)

- **Configuration check (`in_collision(q)`)**: Discretizes link 1 (`[0, 0]` to `e`) into `15` linearly interpolated points and link 2 (`e` to `t`) into `15` points (`30` points total, with `e` duplicated), reporting a collision if any sampled point satisfies $(x > x_0) \land (x < x_1) \land (y > y_0) \land (y < y_1)$ for any box in `OBSTACLES`.
- **Joint-segment check (`edge_free(a, b, step=0.02)`)**: Subdivides segment $\overline{ab}$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$ into $n = \max(2, \lceil \|b - a\|_2 / 0.02 \rceil)$ configurations and verifies that none are in collision.

### 3.3 Joint-Space RRT & Randomized Shortcutting (`rrt`, `shortcut`)

- **Rapidly-exploring Random Tree (`rrt`)**:
  - Searches $C$-space $[-\pi, \pi]^2$ for up to `iters = 3000` iterations with extension step `step = 0.15 rad` and `goal_bias = 0.1` (`10%` probability of sampling `goal`, `90%` uniform on $[-\pi, \pi]^2$).
  - Maintains a pre-allocated array `arr` of shape `(iters + 2, 2)` for vectorized Euclidean nearest-neighbor search `argmin(linalg.norm(arr[:len(nodes)] - x, axis=1))`.
  - Steers from nearest node $q_i$ toward sample $x$ by $q_{\text{new}} = q_i + (x - q_i) \min\!\left(1, \frac{\text{step}}{\|x - q_i\|_2 + 10^{-9}}\right)$.
  - If `edge_free(q_i, q_new)` holds, appends $q_{\text{new}}$ and checks direct goal termination: $\|q_{\text{new}} - q_{\text{goal}}\|_2 < 0.15$ and `edge_free(q_new, q_goal)`.
- **Path shortcutting (`shortcut(path, rng, tries=100)`)**:
  - Performs `100` random shortcut attempts; when `len(path) >= 3`, draws two distinct waypoint indices $i < j$ uniformly without replacement, and if $j - i > 1$ and `edge_free(path[i], path[j])`, replaces `path[i+1:j]` with the direct segment `path[i] -> path[j]`.

## 4. Training

### 4.1 Sampling-Based Planning Benchmark (No Learned Parameters Yet)

`lab-hands` currently implements a **classical geometric motion planner** with zero learned weights. Across the `10` target wells $\times$ `10` RNG seeds (`demo/output.txt`, measured on CPU, 2026-11-18):

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Well index sanity check (`65`) | `F5` (row-major) / `A9` (column-major) | Verifies both SBS 96-well indexing conventions |
| Straight joint-space move blocked | `10 / 10` wells (`100%`) | Reagent bottle `(0.15, 0.27, 0.24, 0.34)` blocks naive linear joint interpolation for every target |
| RRT planning success rate | `100 / 100` trials (`100.0%`) | `10` wells $\times$ `10` seeds (`iters=3000`, `step=0.15 rad`, `goal_bias=0.1`) |
| CPU planning + smoothing latency | `70.6 ms` mean / `278.4 ms` max | Pure `numpy` single-core CPU execution |
| Joint-space trajectory length | `2.44 rad` raw $\to$ `2.08 rad` shortcut | `-14.8%` mean path length reduction from `100` shortcut iterations |

### 4.2 Planned Imitation & Visuo-Tactile Learning Pipeline

As documented in `README.md`, this geometric planner establishes the coordinate frame, obstacle-avoidance baseline, and well-addressing ground truth for two upcoming stages:
1. **3D Physics & Tactile Simulation**: Porting the 96-well pipetting task to MuJoCo (`autobio-bench/pipette-mujoco` / SO-ARM100) with simulated optical tactile pads.
2. **Demonstration-Driven Policy Learning**: Training Action Chunking with Transformers (`ACT`), `Diffusion Policy`, or compact Vision-Language-Action models (`SmolVLA`, `OpenVLA`) fused with self-supervised touch encoders (`Sparsh`) so fine insertion into clear tubes and wells relies on contact shear rather than noisy monocular/RGB-D depth.

## 6. Unique Properties

### Distinctive Invariants & Guarantees

1. **Pure-NumPy, Zero-Simulator Reproducibility**: Runs anywhere Python and `numpy` are installed (`requirements.txt` has a single line: `numpy`), completing 100 seeded obstacle-avoidance planning episodes in ~`7 s` on a single CPU without ROS, MoveIt, OMPL, or GPU drivers.
2. **Explicit SBS Dual-Order Well Addressing**: Directly encodes both row-major (`A1..A12`) and column-major (`A1..H1`) 96-well indexing, surfacing coordinate conventions explicitly before trajectories touch labware.
3. **Guaranteed Non-Triviality**: All `10` benchmark wells fail naive straight-line joint interpolation (`10/10` blocked by the reagent bottle), ensuring every reported success exercises non-convex obstacle avoidance.

### Known Limitations

- **2D projection & zero-radius links**: Models the arm and pipette as infinitely thin 2D line segments sampled at `15` points per link (small obstacle corners can slip between sample points ~`2 cm` apart on `L1 = 0.30 m`), with strict `<` / `>` AABB inequalities and no Z-axis retract-over-plate motion.
- **Euclidean joint metric without $2\pi$ wrapping**: `rrt`, `edge_free`, and `length` treat joint angles in $\mathbb{R}^2$ rather than on the flat torus $\mathbb{T}^2 = S^1 \times S^1$, prohibiting shortest-path wrapping across $\pm\pi$ and leaving `ik()` outputs unnormalized relative to $[-\pi, \pi]$.
- **Single-branch IK goal selection**: `main()` commits to `elbow = +1` whenever that goal configuration is collision-free in isolation, even if `elbow = +1` is disconnected or requires a much longer detour than `elbow = -1`.
- **CI assertion coverage**: `demo/plan_to_well.py` prints summary metrics and exits `0` without asserting `ok == trials` or verifying post-plan trajectory collision freedom.

### Ecosystem Interfaces Across Auto-Lab

- **Sibling `L1` (`stepbot`)**: [`stepbot`](https://github.com/yaniguan/stepbot) compiles high-level liquid-handling steps (`transfer`, `distribute`, `mix`, `dilution_series`) for Cartesian gantry robots (Opentrons OT-2), whereas `lab-hands` handles articulated-arm motion planning and contact-rich vessel manipulation around non-deck obstacles.
- **Upstream from `L2` (`lab-ears`) & `L3` (`YLM`)**: Target wells, reagent vessels, and volume/speed constraints extracted by [`lab-ears`](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-ears) and planned by [`YLM`](https://github.com/yaniguan/YLM) parameterize `lab-hands` manipulation goals.
- **Integration with `L4` (`lab-rehearsal`, `lab-verify`) & `L0` (`lab-feet`)**: Planned trajectories feed [`lab-rehearsal`](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-rehearsal) for pre-execution collision/clearance rehearsal, while learned visuomotor/tactile policies will be compressed via [`lab-feet`](https://github.com/yaniguan/lab-feet) (`int8`/`4-bit` / `Embodied.cpp`) for low-latency control loops at the bench.
